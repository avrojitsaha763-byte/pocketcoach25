# ⚡ LEVEL UP: Solo Leveling System Fitness Engine

> *"The System has chosen you as a Player. Will you accept the Awakening?"*

A market-disrupting, gamified fitness application inspired by the **Solo Leveling Rank-Up System**. Transforms mundane daily workouts into high-stakes Hunter missions featuring holographic HUDs, procedural synth audio, camera-based AI form referees, dungeon gate raids, and shadow soldier extractions.

---

1. **⚡ Holographic Hunter HUD & Status Window:** Real-time STR, AGI, VIT, INT, and PER stat allocation with interactive radar charts and XP progression.
2. **📜 Daily Quest: "Preparing to Become Strong":** 100 Push-ups, 100 Sit-ups, 100 Squats, and 10km Cardio tracked in real time.
3. **⚠️ Penalty Zone Survival Protocol:** Miss your midnight quest deadline and trigger a 4-minute emergency Desert Centipede HIIT drill with siren alarms.
4. **🖤 "ARISE" Shadow Extraction Vault:** Defeat bad habits and physical milestones, then cast **[ARISE]** to forge loyal Shadow Soldiers (Igris, Iron, Tank, Beru) with permanent passive stat multipliers.
5. **🚪 Dungeon Gate Raids & Boss Battles:** Battle 6 ranks of dungeon bosses (E-Rank Goblin Den to S-Rank Frost Monarch Red Gate) where every push-up, squat, or sprint strikes the boss's HP bar.
6. **👁️ Computer Vision AI Camera Rep Counter:** WebRTC webcam motion analysis that tracks exercise depth and validates reps hands-free with real-time audio confirmation.
7. **🔮 Interactive Hunter Skill Tree:** Unlock active and passive skills (Sprint, Iron Will, Stealth Focus, Ruler's Authority, Monarch's Domain).
8. **🧪 Potion Alchemy & Nutrition Engine:** Real-time fluid physics flask for hydration tracking alongside Calorie (HP) and Protein (MP) synthesis bars.
9. **📦 Blessed Random Box & Cursed Relic Gacha:** Earn random reward boxes containing elixirs, titles, and artifact accessories.
10. **🧘 Sanctuary Chamber (4-7-8 Recovery):** Bioluminescent expanding breathwork guide paired with procedural 432Hz alpha wave focus synthesizer.
11. **🔊 Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synth Engine:** High-fidelity sci-fi chimes, level-up fanfares, klaxon alarms, and bass drops synthesized directly in the browser without external audio files.
12. **🤖 The Architect AI System Overseer:** Generates cold, authoritative, authentic Solo Leveling system decrees and workout evaluations.
13. **⚔️ Critical Strike Combat Calculations:** Perception (PER) and Strength (STR) attributes dynamically scale critical hit chance and damage output against dungeon bosses.
14. **💾 Offline-First Persistent Storage:** Auto-saves hunter level, inventory, shadow roster, and attributes locally with zero cloud delay.
15. **📱 Pure Vanilla Tech Stack:** High performance, zero build step required, 60 FPS canvas particle physics, instant cross-device compatibility.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Option 1: Direct File Launch
Double click [`index.html`](./index.html) in any modern browser!

### Option 2: Python Local Server
```bash
python server.py
```
App will open automatically at `http://localhost:8080`.
